Debate toolkit

Debate Knowledge

Start with the basics and gradually build habits of arguing, listening, and responding. Refer to the competition rules for specific formats.

Common debate terms

Case
Build a clear argument around your side's position, setting out definitions, criteria, reasoning, and the logic that connects them.
Cross-examination
Test the premises of an argument with targeted questions and clarify disagreements, rather than simply asking more questions.
Attack & defense
Respond to the other side's arguments, defend your own case, and explain why the clash matters to the final judgment.
Closing statement
Summarize the key disputes and comparison criteria, and explain how your case supports the conclusion.
